I can't connect my Google account.

Sign in with the Google account that has access to your AdMob account, and make sure you allow the AdMob read-only permission on Google's consent screen. If you see an error, sign out in Settings, sign in again, and send us the exact error text if it continues.

We will never ask for your Google password or sign-in tokens. Please do not send them.

My numbers look different from AdMob.

Earnings Lens shows estimated earnings from the Google AdMob API. Recent figures can be delayed or adjusted by Google, and final payments may differ. Pull to refresh in the app, and check your AdMob account for official figures.

Widgets or Apple Watch aren't updating.

Open Earnings Lens on your iPhone once so it can refresh your data, and make sure your Apple Watch is paired and nearby. iOS and watchOS control how often widgets refresh, so updates may take a little time.

Restore or cancel my subscription.

To restore, open the Pro screen in the app and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the Apple ID you used to buy. To cancel, go to iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Refunds can be requested at Apple's Report a Problem. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

Delete my data and connection.

  1. Sign out: In the app, go to Settings → Account → Sign Out. This deletes stored sign-in tokens and cached earnings on your iPhone, widgets and Apple Watch.
  2. Revoke Google access: Remove Earnings Lens at Google Account → Third-party connections.
  3. Calendar event: Turn off Sync to iOS Calendar in Settings to remove the synced event, or delete it in the Calendar app.
  4. Delete the app: Removes the app's local data from your device.
  5. Support emails: Email a deletion request and tell us which messages or information to delete.

Earnings Lens has no accounts of its own, and the developer cannot access data on your devices remotely. See the Privacy Policy for details.
