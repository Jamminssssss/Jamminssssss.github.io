Earnings Lens Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Earnings Lens: Ad Revenue ("Earnings Lens", "the app", "we") handles information when you use the app on iPhone, Apple Watch and widgets.

Developer: Jae-min Park · Effective date: November 10, 2026

In short.

Earnings Lens does not run its own servers, does not collect analytics, does not track you and does not sell or share your data. Your AdMob data goes straight from Google to your devices.

Information accessedHow it is usedStorageLimited UseDeletionContact

1. Information the app accesses

Google account data

When you sign in with Google, Earnings Lens requests:

  • Basic profile (name, email address, profile picture): used only to show which Google account is signed in.
  • AdMob read-only access (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admob.readonly): used to read your AdMob publisher account details and network/mediation reports, such as estimated earnings, ad requests, impressions, match rate and eCPM, broken down by date, app, ad unit and ad network.

Earnings Lens uses read-only access only. It cannot change your AdMob account, ads or payment settings.

Purchase information

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. The app checks your subscription status with Apple's StoreKit. We never receive your payment details.

2. How the information is used

Google user data is used only to provide the app's user-facing features:

  • Showing your earnings and performance metrics in the app
  • Showing earnings on Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and on Apple Watch
  • Daily revenue alarms (local notifications created on your device)
  • Optional calendar sync: if you turn it on, the app creates or updates one event in your device calendar showing today's earnings

3. Storage and security

  • Data is requested directly from Google's APIs over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored in the iOS/watchOS Keychain on your devices.
  • Recent earnings summaries are cached on your device (in the app's shared container) so widgets and Apple Watch can display them. If you use Apple Watch, earnings data and the access needed to refresh it are sent from your iPhone to your paired Apple Watch over Apple's WatchConnectivity and stored in the Watch Keychain.
  • Your Google user data is not uploaded to the developer or to any third-party server.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share or transfer your Google user data or any other personal information to third parties. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI or machine-learning models.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

Earnings Lens' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Data stays on your devices only as long as you stay signed in.
  • Signing out in the app deletes stored tokens and cached earnings data, including on Apple Watch.
  • Deleting the app removes its local data from your device.
  • You can revoke Earnings Lens' access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • If you turned on calendar sync, you can delete the calendar event in your Calendar app, or turn the feature off in Earnings Lens.

7. Device permissions

  • Notifications: only for revenue alarms you set.
  • Calendar: only if you turn on calendar sync.

You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

8. Tracking and analytics

Earnings Lens does not include analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

9. Children

Earnings Lens is a business tool for AdMob publishers. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no analytics scripts, ads or cookies of its own, but GitHub may process connection information such as IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact jmpark3758@gmail.com.
