A SMALLER DATA FOOTPRINT

From Google to your device.
Nowhere else.

Earnings Lens has no developer-run servers and no accounts of its own. It requests read-only AdMob access (admob.readonly) and your basic Google profile. Sign-in tokens are kept in the iOS Keychain.

Your AdMob data is never sold, shared, used for advertising or used to train AI models. Earnings Lens has no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions?

Can Earnings Lens change my AdMob account?

No. The app only requests the read-only AdMob scope. It cannot change ads, settings or payments.

Do I need to enter my Google password?

No. Sign-in happens on Google's own sign-in screen. Earnings Lens never sees or stores your Google password.

Are the numbers exact?

They are estimated earnings reported by the Google AdMob API. They can be delayed or adjusted later and may differ from your final AdMob payments.

How do I remove my data?

Sign out in Settings to delete stored sign-in tokens and cached earnings on your iPhone, widgets and Apple Watch. You can also revoke access in your Google account. See Support & Data Deletion.
