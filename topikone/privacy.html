Privacy Policy

Topik One · Operated by Jae-min Park (independent developer) · Effective date: November 7, 2026

In short

Topik One does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email or phone number. Your study data stays on your device and in your own private iCloud. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, and we use Google Firebase for anonymous usage analytics and crash reports. We never sell your personal information.

1. Information we use and why

Study data

Your exam progress, scores, incorrect-answer notes, vocabulary and grammar bookmarks, and the last lesson you studied are stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, this data is also synced to the private CloudKit database and iCloud key-value storage of your own Apple account so it can be restored after reinstalling or on another device. The developer cannot access your private iCloud data.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. The app checks your subscription status with Apple (StoreKit) to unlock Premium features and remove ads.

Notifications

If you turn on subscription reminders and allow notifications, the app schedules local notifications on your device (for example, before a free trial ends). These are created on your device and are not sent from our servers.

Usage analytics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used (for example, which screens are opened, questions answered, and whether you are a free or Premium user, along with your app language), and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports with device and app diagnostic information. Firebase Remote Config is used to adjust app settings and promotions without an update. This information is used to fix bugs and improve the app and is not linked to your name or contact details.

Advertising

The free version shows banner and app-open ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect device information, an advertising identifier, IP address and ad interaction data to serve, measure and limit ads. Topik One does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, so on iOS the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not shared with advertisers for tracking across other companies' apps and websites. Premium subscribers do not see ads.

3. Retention and deletion

  • On-device data is removed when you delete the app.
  • iCloud data can be deleted in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage (or by turning off iCloud for Topik One and deleting its data).
  • Analytics data is retained by Google Firebase for up to 14 months; crash reports are retained according to Firebase's policies (generally 90 days).
  • To ask us to delete analytics data associated with your app installation, email us (see below).

4. Your choices and rights

  • You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and Apple Advertising.
  • You can turn notifications off at any time in Settings → Notifications → Topik One.
  • Subscribing to Premium removes ads.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/UK under GDPR, California under CCPA, or Korea under PIPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your personal information. Contact us to exercise these rights.

5. Children

Topik One is a general-audience educational app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Security

Data is transmitted using encrypted connections (HTTPS) provided by Apple and Google. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we use the protections offered by these platforms.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated, and significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
