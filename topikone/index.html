Prepare for TOPIK I with confidence.

Topik One helps beginner Korean learners practice for TOPIK I (Levels 1–2) with exam-style reading and listening questions, full listening scripts, and clear explanations in your own language.

What's inside

Reading practice

Real exam-style TOPIK I reading questions with easy-to-understand explanations.

Listening training

Listening questions with full scripts so you can follow every word and improve step by step.

Vocabulary & grammar

Essential beginner vocabulary and basic grammar to build a strong foundation, all the way toward TOPIK II.

Review your mistakes

Incorrect answers are saved to a review note, and progress statistics show how far you've come.

iCloud sync

Your progress and bookmarks sync through your own iCloud account, so they come back after reinstalling or on another device.
