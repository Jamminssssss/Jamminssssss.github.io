Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Open the Premium screen in the app and tap Restore. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone or iPad, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Topik One, or open apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid being charged for the next one.

How do I request a refund?

Payments are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My study progress disappeared after reinstalling the app.

Progress, incorrect-answer notes and bookmarks are synced through your private iCloud. Check that you're signed in to iCloud and that iCloud is enabled for Topik One in Settings → [your name] → iCloud. Open the app and wait a moment on Wi-Fi for the data to restore.

Listening audio doesn't play.

Check that your device isn't in silent mode and the volume is up, then try closing and reopening the app. If it still doesn't play, email us with the exam and question number.

I found a mistake in a question or explanation.

Thank you! Please email us the exam, section (Reading/Listening) and question number, and what looks wrong. We fix reported issues in upcoming updates.

Which languages are explanations available in?

English, Chinese (Simplified & Traditional), French, Indonesian, Japanese, Spanish, Thai, Vietnamese and Korean. The app follows your device language.
