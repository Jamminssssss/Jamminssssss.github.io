Privacy Policy

JLPT Prep · Jae-min Park, independent developer · Effective November 9, 2026

JLPT Prep requires no account and never asks for your name, email or phone number. Your study data stays on your device. The free version shows ads via Google AdMob, which may use your advertising identifier only if you allow tracking. We do not run our own servers and never sell your personal information.

1. Information we use and why

Study data

Your reading and listening progress, answers, vocabulary progress, display settings and the number of free questions you've used are stored only on your device. This data is not sent to the developer or to any server.

Calendar (optional)

If you allow calendar access, the app may add study-record events to its own calendar on your device. It does not read, change or upload your other calendar events, and you can turn off access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. The app checks your subscription status with Apple (StoreKit) to unlock content for each JLPT level and remove ads.

Advertising and tracking

The free version shows banner, interstitial and app-open ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information, IP address and ad interaction data to serve, measure and limit ads.

iOS asks whether you allow JLPT Prep to track your activity (App Tracking Transparency). If you allow tracking, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) may be used by Google to show personalized ads and measure ad performance across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the IDFA is not shared and you may still see non-personalized ads. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Subscribers see no ads.

3. Retention and deletion

  • On-device data is removed when you delete the app.
  • Calendar events created by the app can be deleted in the Calendar app.
  • Advertising data collected by Google is retained under Google's policies.

4. Your choices and rights

  • Allow or deny tracking any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and manage ad personalization in Apple Advertising.
  • Subscribing to a level removes ads.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, under GDPR, CCPA or Korea's PIPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your personal information. Contact us to exercise these rights.

5. Children

JLPT Prep is rated 4+ on the App Store and is suitable for learners of all ages. It is a general-audience educational app for language learners rather than an app made specifically for children (it is not in the App Store Kids category). The app has no account sign-up and does not ask anyone, including children, for personal information such as name, email or phone number. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child's information has been collected, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) provided by Apple and Google. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we rely on the protections these platforms offer.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date above will be updated, and significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
