Features

  • Five levelsChoose N5 (Beginner) through N1 (Advanced) and study only what you need.
  • ReadingJLPT-style reading questions with passages, materials you can enlarge, and explanations.

  • VocabularyLevel-by-level vocabulary with meanings and sentence audio.
  • WritingTrace kanji and kana stroke by stroke with a stroke-order guide.
