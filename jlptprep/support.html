FAQ

How do subscriptions work?

Each JLPT level (N1, N2, N3, N4 and N5) has its own monthly or yearly subscription. A level's subscription unlocks all of that level's reading and listening sets, explanations, scripts and vocabulary, and removes ads. You can try a few questions in each section for free before subscribing.

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Open the subscription screen for your level in the app and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → JLPT Prep, or open apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid being charged for the next one.

How do I request a refund?

Payments are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My progress disappeared after reinstalling.

Study progress is stored only on your device, so it is removed when the app is deleted. Your subscriptions are tied to your Apple ID and can be restored at any time with Restore Purchase.

Listening audio doesn't play.

Check that silent mode is off and the volume is up. The first time you open a listening set, the app prepares its audio, which can take a moment. If it still doesn't play, close and reopen the app, or email us with the level, set and question number.

I found a mistake in a question.

Thank you! Email us the level, section (Reading, Listening, Vocabulary or Grammar), set and question number, and what looks wrong. Reported issues are fixed in upcoming updates.

Which languages are supported?

English, Chinese (Simplified & Traditional), French, Indonesian, Japanese, Spanish, Thai, Vietnamese and Korean. The app follows your device language.
